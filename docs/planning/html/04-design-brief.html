IN ONE MINUTE
  • A fresh, clean and colourful look: a white page, borderless cards on soft shadows, big product photos, round store logos.
  • One green for every action, soft green for “cheapest”, orange for deals. Small, quiet type.
  • Right to left, phone first. The same part looks the same on every page.
Banner bright photo, calm green panel, white button
Quick search square product photos
Store row round logo, cheapest on pale green
Bottom bar floating capsule, raised scan button

02 Type, layout and the main pieces

ElementRule
TypeRubik, small and quiet: 11 to 14 px does most of the work; text fields are 16 px so phones never zoom. Right to left. Prices always ₪ with two digits.
Cards and spacingWhite, no outline, soft shadow, corners 10 to 20 px, spacing in steps of 4 px. Touch targets at least 44 px; the notch and home indicator are respected.
Header and bottom barA flat white header (logo, city chip, basket pill, a search icon once the page’s search box scrolls away). A floating white capsule with Home, a raised green Scan circle, and Basket.
Banner and quick searchThree slides (compare, scan, deals) with a bright photo, a calm green panel, white text and button. Ten square product photos for quick search.
Product and deal cardsA big photo, name, “cheapest at” with the chain’s round logo, price per 100 g or litre when useful, a green add button. Deal cards add a saving pill (for example -40%).
Store rowThe same on the product and basket pages: round logo, branch, price, gap from cheapest; the cheapest on pale green with a tag; promotion line and “old price” warning.
FeelItems fly to the basket icon, loading is a calm shimmer, pages fade in. Keyboard focus rings and labels on every icon button.
